## Collectify 0.18.0 — Island Care 🏝️

Installs over your existing Collectify; your data is kept.

### New: Island Care (the new **Island** tab)
A small 3D island you look after. **Scan cards, grow food, care for your island Pokémon.** Check in for two minutes, make your buddy happy, leave.

- **Pick a buddy** (Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle), name it, and follow a ten-step tutorial: plant → water → harvest → craft → feed → play.
- **A real 3D island:** low-poly look, animated water with foam, a sky that follows your phone's clock (sunrise, day, sunset, night with lantern glow and fireflies). Tap the ground to walk, tap anything to walk up and use it, drag to orbit, pinch to zoom. Things between the camera and you fade out instead of blocking the view.
- **Pokémon with depth and personality.** Each Pokémon is built into a chunky 3D model from its sprite (with an outline), so it has real thickness as you orbit. They wander, hop, eat from the bowl, play with toys and fall asleep in a corner at night. Happy ones give off little type effects (sparks, embers, bubbles, leaves…).
- **They talk.** Speech bubbles pop up with the noise they make — "Pika pika!", "Bulba bulba!", "Psy… duck?" — and change with mood (hungry, thirsty, bored, sleepy, delighted when petted). Each species has its own little synth voice; turn the sounds off in Island → Menu → Settings and you keep the bubbles.
- **Eye button → first person.** Look around with a drag and walk with the on-screen stick. Optionally turn on *first-person interactions*: the camera dives in when you plant, water or feed, and you hold your finger down to do it. Third person always works and is never required.
- **Needs, no pet death.** Hunger, thirst and fun drain slowly in real time. Pokémon eat, drink and play from the dishes and toy box on their own, so twice-a-day check-ins keep them happy. After a long absence they're only a little sad and wait for you — never worse.
- **Six types** (Grass, Fire, Water, Electric, Rock, Psychic) with their own berries, essence, toys, preferred food and drops; eighteen Pokémon to adopt as your slots grow.
- **Progression:** levels unlock plots, workbench slots, Pokémon slots, auto-waterer, the market (level 10: happy Pokémon leave drops you can sell) and the maid station (level 12: maids water, harvest, feed, play and collect for a daily salary), plus ten upgrades, five decorations and an island expansion. Daily quests, weekly goals and streaks.
- **Scanning helps the island:** adding a card unlocks that type's seed, gives essence and XP, a bond boost if it matches one of your island Pokémon, and a bonus for rare cards.
- **Coins are your Treasure Box coins**, so island income buys packs.
- **Reminders (Android):** a quiet notification when a Pokémon needs you or crops are ready, plus a gentle "we miss you" a day later — never overnight. Off switch in Island → Menu → Settings.

### Also
- Bundles three.js r160 (MIT) for the island; it only loads when you open the Island tab.

### Tested, and what wasn't
Played through on an Android emulator and a desktop browser: the whole tutorial, first-person planting, market, maids, drops and scanning bonus, and a real reminder alarm firing and posting its notification. Not tested on a physical phone, so frame rate on older phones is unmeasured (Effects → Lite lowers the island's resolution and frame rate). The Pokémon cries are a simple synthesizer, not recordings, and I could only check that they run without errors, not how they sound.
