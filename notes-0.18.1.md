## Collectify 0.18.1 — Treasure Box balance

Installs over your existing Collectify; your data is kept.

### Easier ways to get packs
- **Free daily pack — choose 1 of 3.** In **Daily**, pick one of three packs from three different games (a new set of three each day, the same all day so you can't re-roll). It goes straight to your vault, ready to rip. The Daily button shows a ! when it's waiting.
- **Pack tokens bank up.** Tokens used to be one per visit-hour; now up to 3 hours of tokens wait for you while you're away. Token prices dropped too (most packs cost 1–2).
- **New: box tickets 🎫.** A ticket type just for boxes. Spend 4–14 (by the box's grade) in *Pick packs → Boxes* to open a whole box — 12 packs — with no mining. Earn them from every 3rd daily-chest day, every 5th Who's-that-Pokémon streak, or trade 8 pack tokens for one (Hourly sheet). Your ticket count sits next to your tokens.

### Cheaper
- Claiming a pack costs about 40% fewer coins (boxes too), breaking a block pays a bit more, recycling dupes pays 6 coins per $1 (was 5), upgrades cost roughly 20–30% less per level, the pickaxe costs 40 (was 50) and you start with 150 coins (was 120).

### Harder blocks, and the pickaxe finally matters
- Every block has about 1.6× the HP.
- **Pickaxe grade vs block grade:** a pickaxe below a block's grade bites it ×0.6 slower for each grade short (a Wooden pickaxe on a Copper block is ×0.6, on Iron ×0.36 …). At or above the block's grade you're a little faster. You see the penalty (⛏ ×0.6) on each block in *Pick packs* and on the block you're mining, and the time estimate includes it. Upgrading raises the pickaxe's grade, which is what makes the big blocks practical — and boxes in rock above your grade are exactly what tickets are for.
- Blocks already in your mine keep the HP they had; the grade penalty applies to them too.

### Honest limits
Balance is by arithmetic and a play-through of the screens on an emulator and a desktop browser, not weeks of real play, so numbers may need another nudge once people use it.
