## Collectify 0.16.0

Installs over your existing Collectify; your data is kept.

### Treasure Box
- **Foil packs, sliced open.** Packs now have a crinkled metallic foil wrapper with ridged crimped seals. Swipe a finger across the top (either direction): a glowing blade follows your finger with sparks, light leaks out of the slit, and the top peels away.
- **3D, "pop" cards.** Revealed cards tilt in 3D under your finger and the artwork lifts off the frame with a shadow and parallax. Rare cards shimmer, and ultra/secret rares are animated on their own. Hold your finger on a card to make the foil, sparkles and art come alive.
- **Hourly pack tokens.** Mint one token an hour; spend tokens in "Pick packs" to buy a pack instantly (boxes still have to be mined).
- **Who's that Pokémon?** A new silhouette challenge every hour (Pokédex gen 1-3, bundled offline). A correct answer pays coins; get one right every hour and the multiplier grows from x1 up to x4. One missed hour is forgiven; a wrong answer resets the streak.
- **Shop.** Buy sleeves (packs of 10), binder cover styles and sticker sets with game coins.

### Binders
- **Customize covers.** Pick a cover style (leather, galaxy, gold foil, holo...), feature cards from the binder, and place stickers (including 151 Pokédex sprites). Drag, resize and rotate everything. Covers show on your shelf.
- **Sleeves.** Sleeve any card from the card viewer (🛡 Sleeve). Sleeved cards get a coloured edge and sheen in the binder and viewer, and a matching card back.

### Trade
- **Digital cards can be traded.** Treasure Box cards can be offered, clearly marked 🎲 DIGITAL with a note that they have no physical equivalent: nothing is handed over in person for them, they move between binders in the app, and they are left out of the market-value totals. Physical cards are labelled "exchanged in person".

### Look and feel
- Frosted-glass header and tab bar, spring-in sheets, glossier buttons, glass cards and screen transitions.

Notes: tested on an Android emulator and a desktop browser, not on a physical phone. Offline two-code trading is still physical-only.
