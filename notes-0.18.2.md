## Collectify 0.18.2 — Island Care: every type plays at the same pace

Installs over your existing Collectify; your data is kept.

### Fix
- **Crops ripen in the same time for every type.** Berries used to take 25 minutes (Grass) up to 60 (Psychic), and any berry makes Basic Food, so a Grass starter could play much faster than a Fire or Water one. All six types now take 25 minutes, so your starter doesn't decide how much you can play. A type's identity is still its own berry, toy, preferred food and drop.
- Crops you've already planted use the new time straight away.

Checked in the Node simulation and by reading the built APK's data file; I did not replay the tutorial on a device for this one-number change.
