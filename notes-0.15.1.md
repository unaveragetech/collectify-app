## Collectify 0.15.1

Installs over your existing Collectify; your data is kept.

### Treasure Box
- **Boxes are now a real grind.** A box is one *hardened* block for every pack inside it (12), mined one after another. Each block you break gives you one pack to rip, so a box no longer pays out all its packs for a single block. Products with no market price are valued sensibly (a box is worth a box of packs), so a cheap box now costs 180 coins instead of 8.
- Boxes show their pack-blocks as pips and a stack of waiting blocks behind the active one.
- **Much more visual mine:** glowing crystals, light shafts, an animated rainbow frame, impact shockwaves, critical hits, tap combos (more damage the longer your streak), a pickaxe motion trail, blocks that squash and glow before they break, and coins that fly out when they do.
- **Animated pack-opening backdrop:** an aurora, spinning rays and rising orbs that change colour with each card's rarity and pulse on good pulls.

### Trade
- The trade screens now show a step tracker (Connect, Add cards, Agree, Swap, Complete) with where you are, plus a "Good to know before you trade" section covering Wi-Fi, no money moving through the app, agreement resets, estimated values, and what completing does.

Note: tested on an Android emulator and a desktop browser, not yet on a physical phone.
