## Collectify 0.18.3 — choose which game your daily pack comes from

Installs over your existing Collectify; your data is kept.

### Fix
- **Daily pack: pick your game.** The free daily pack used to come from a fixed mix of games, so a Pokémon collector could be handed Lorcana or Star Wars with no way out. The Daily pack screen now has a row of games along the top: **🎲 Mix**, Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Lorcana, One Piece, Digimon, Star Wars and Flesh & Blood.
- Choose a single game and you get **three different packs from that game**. Choose Mix for three games like before. Your choice is remembered (Pokémon until you change it).
- You still get one pack a day: the three options for each game are fixed for the day, so flipping between games to re-roll doesn't work.
- Only sets with enough cards to open are offered, so a game with none shows a short explanation instead of an empty screen.

Checked by flipping between games, taking a pack and confirming the choice stuck in a desktop browser; the built app was installed in the emulator but I didn't repeat the daily-pack screen there.
