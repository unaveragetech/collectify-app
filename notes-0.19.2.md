## Collectify 0.19.2 — binders are filled from Scan only

Installs over your existing Collectify; your data is kept.

- **Wishlist:** the "＋ Add to a binder" button on each wish is gone. A wish still shows "✓ You have this in a binder" once you own it, and is crossed off automatically when you add it from Scan.
- **Browse:** a card's detail sheet no longer has an Add to binder button; it now offers **♡ Add to wishlist** and Close. Tapping a printing in "Other printings" or swiping through them opens that printing's details instead of adding it.
- Cards go into binders from the **Scan** tab (camera or Type), as well as through trades and Treasure Box pulls you keep. The empty-binder hint says so.

### Tested, and what wasn't
Checked in a desktop browser that the detail sheet shows the new buttons and no binder button, and that wishlist rows have no add button. Not retested on a phone.
